## What is ORDERiQ?

ORDERiQ is a **smart WhatsApp ordering assistant** built specifically for Pakistani restaurants. It lets your customers browse your menu, place orders, and get support — all through WhatsApp, the app they already use every day.

Think of it as a **virtual order-taker** that never sleeps, never takes a break, and can handle hundreds of customers at the same time — while you focus on cooking great food.

> **No app downloads required.** Customers just message your restaurant's WhatsApp number and start ordering.

## 📱 WhatsApp Ordering — How It Works

### For Your Customers:
1. **Customer messages your WhatsApp number** — says "Hi", "AOA", "menu dikhao", or anything
2. **AI greets them warmly** — in their language (English or Urdu), by name if they've ordered before
3. **Customer browses the menu** — through easy-to-tap category buttons or just types what they want
4. **Items are added to cart** — customer can add, remove, or change quantities anytime
5. **AI suggests add-ons** — "Would you like a drink with that?" (increases your revenue!)
6. **Customer picks order type** — Delivery, Pickup, or Dine-in
7. **Order summary shown** — full breakdown with prices, delivery fee, and total
8. **Customer confirms** — order is placed and your kitchen is notified instantly

### For You (The Restaurant Owner):
- Orders appear on your **live dashboard** in real time
- You can update order status (Received → Preparing → Out for Delivery → Delivered)
- Customer is **automatically notified** at each stage via WhatsApp

## 🛵 Three Ways to Fulfill Orders

### 1. Delivery 🏠
- Customer provides delivery address
- AI checks if the address is **within your delivery zones**
- Enforces **minimum order amount** for delivery
- Adds **delivery fee** automatically
- Shows **estimated delivery time**
- Returning customers can type **"same address"** to reuse their saved address

### 2. Pickup 🏃
- Customer picks a collection time
- AI validates the time is **within your operating hours**
- No delivery fee charged
- Shows **estimated prep time**

### 3. Dine-in 🍽️
- Customer provides **number of guests** and **reservation time**
- AI validates everything against your operating hours
- Provides your dine-in contact number for any questions

## 👨‍💼 Human Handoff — AI Knows When to Step Aside

The AI is smart enough to know when a **human should take over**:

### AI Automatically Transfers to Staff When:
- Customer explicitly asks to **speak to a human** ("manager se baat karni hai", "talk to someone")
- Customer sounds **frustrated or angry** (AI detects sentiment)
- Customer has a **complex issue** the AI can't handle
- Customer has given **3 invalid inputs** in a row

### What Happens During Handoff:
1. Customer gets a message: *"I'm transferring you to the restaurant manager now. A human team member will reply shortly."*
2. **Staff gets a high-priority alert** on the dashboard
3. Conversation switches to **human mode** — AI stops replying
4. Staff replies directly through the dashboard
5. When done, staff switches back to **AI mode** — AI resumes handling
